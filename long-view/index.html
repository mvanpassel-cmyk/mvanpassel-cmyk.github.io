News for the long now

The Long View

Most news is forgotten by next week. This page keeps only the stories people will still find significant in 02126: turning points in climate, life, science and how we live together.

How long will it matter? The scale runs from 10 to 10,000 years ahead, each step ten times longer. Tap a band to filter.

How stories are chosen

Every morning about two dozen science, health, environment and world news feeds are read. An AI editor asks one question of each item: will this still matter in 50 years? Most days nothing passes. The headlines and reasons here are written fresh; follow the source for the original reporting.

Where the idea comes from

Kirk Citron's The Long News (TED, 2010), later a Long Now Foundation project, collected stories that would resonate for decades or centuries. It is no longer kept up. This is a new take on it.
