How to find more that work

  • One steady groove plus one free-floating part. A track with a fixed beat, layered with music that has no strict tempo (modal jazz solos, ambient pads, a solo instrument), almost always fits. You never have to match BPM.
  • Modal or one-chord music is forgiving. Tracks that sit on one scale for minutes (Kind of Blue, Eno, drones) clash with very little. Songs with lots of chord changes clash with almost everything.
  • Same key, or a key a fifth apart. If both tracks are listed in the same key (or e.g. C and G), they'll blend. Sites like tunebat.com list the key and BPM for most pop and electronic tracks.
  • Tempo off by a few %? Use the speed control on one deck (0.25 steps on most videos, finer on some), then nudge the offset until the beats lock.
  • Turn one deck down. A duet usually works best with one track leading and the other at 40–60% volume.
