SPAR Good Practices

The 35 indicators of the WHO IHR States Parties Self-Assessment Annual Reporting tool (SPAR, 2nd edition), with literature case studies and good practices, the matching JEE indicators with countries that score high, and the matching IHR benchmarks.

SPAR data and documents
  • SPAR questionnaire, 2nd edition (PDF, WHO). For each indicator countries can tick “strength/best practice” next to their comments, so a country's own SPAR report may name good practices.
  • e-SPAR public pages: scores per capacity, by country, region and year; export to Excel. Indicator-level scores are not public.
  • WHO Global Health Observatory: SPAR average per country, with API (SDG 3.d.1).
