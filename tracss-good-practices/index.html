The survey and its documents (sources and how to read this)

TrACSS is the yearly self-assessment on national action plans on antimicrobial resistance, run since 2017 by the Tripartite (FAO, OIE, WHO) and since 2022 by the Quadripartite (FAO, UNEP, WHO and WOAH); WHO administers it. Each country submits one consolidated answer agreed by all sectors. This page uses the ninth round: questionnaire version 9.0 (launched April 2025, responses due June 2025) and the data export of 1 October 2025, covering 184 countries. The 2024 UN political declaration on AMR sets a target of 95% of countries answering every year by 2030.

  • TrACSS Global Database: country results, the questionnaire and the data download.
  • WHO: 2025 edition of the survey launches. Questions to tracss@who.int.

Rated questions use five levels (A to E); 5.7 and 5.9 use four (A to D). A higher level assumes the lower ones are reached (a country choosing D should also meet B and C). The wording of each level differs per question; open a question to see it.

How to read this. Answers are self-reported by national focal points and are not checked externally; two countries choosing the same level can be in different situations. Percentages are of countries in the comparison group that answered the question. The crosswalk to JEE, SPAR and PHEPA per topic is our own and not official. Literature takeaways are our own words; items marked as not read were listed from titles only.
Questions and answer options are taken from the TrACSS 2025 questionnaire (version 9.0) and the data export of 1 October 2025 published by the Quadripartite; some long answer texts are shortened in the bars but shown in full under each question. Free-text answers and uploaded files are left out; links countries gave to their national documents are shown. Built with Claude for Mark van Passel. Check details before citing.
