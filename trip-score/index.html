How it works

Every note is composed live on your phone and played on recorded instruments (strings, harp, piano, brass, woodwinds, percussion), so the music never repeats. Fifteen moods, from Dreamy to Noir; swipe the row for more. They cross-fade slowly, like a cue changing in a film. Shuffle picks a new one every few minutes. Keep presence low: the idea is that you barely notice it, until you do.

Screen off: live mode keeps playing in most cases. If iOS stops it when the screen locks, turn on Lock-screen loop: it composes a 2½-minute seamless loop of the current mood and plays it through the phone's media player, which never stops. Use the lock-screen skip buttons to change mood.

Recording saves what you heard (live mode) as an audio file, so a trip keeps its own score.

Works offline once loaded (about 15 MB of instruments, downloaded once). Tip: Share → Add to Home Screen.

Instruments: real orchestral recordings from VSCO 2 Community Edition by Versilian Studios, released under CC0.
