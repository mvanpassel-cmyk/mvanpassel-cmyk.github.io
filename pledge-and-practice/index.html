Implementation tracker · Dutch Global Health Strategy 2023–2030

Pledge & Practice

The Netherlands promised in 2022 to work "together for health worldwide". The strategy came without indicators, so this page builds the missing framework: every pledge, traced through three progress reports and the documents around them.
