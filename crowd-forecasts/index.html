Biggest moves this week

How to read this. A price of 30¢ on a “Yes” share roughly means the crowd puts the chance at 30%. The date is the market's deadline: the latest moment the answer will be known, sometimes earlier. For multi-option markets the leading option is shown; for “by date” markets, the nearest deadline is shown with later ones below.

Caveats. Polymarket and Kalshi trade real money (volume in dollars or contracts); Manifold uses play money (shown as number of traders). Metaculus is not a market: it aggregates individual forecasts, weighted towards recent ones, and is often better calibrated on long-range questions. Thin markets can be moved by a single trader, Kalshi and PredictIt lean towards US questions, and market wording and resolution rules matter: open the market before quoting a number.

Data: Polymarket, Kalshi, Manifold and Metaculus, PredictIt and Smarkets. Selection is automatic, by trading volume and topic keywords.
