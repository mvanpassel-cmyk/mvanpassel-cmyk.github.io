Impact classes

  1. Minor: one small organisation, under 10,000 people.
  2. Limited: up to 1 million people, local disruption, under $10M.
  3. Significant: 1–10 million people, services down for days, a national chain hit, $10–100M.
  4. Major: 10–100 million people, hospitals, energy, telecom or government disrupted, $100M–1B, state espionage.
  5. Severe: over 100 million people, several countries disrupted, over $1B, or risk to life.

Claims by criminal groups that the victim has not confirmed are marked "claimed" and stay at class 3 or lower.

Where it comes from

Every four hours: a dozen security news feeds (The Record, BleepingComputer, DataBreaches.net, SecurityWeek, Security.nl and others), read by an AI editor that merges headlines into incidents and scores them; confirmed breaches from Have I Been Pwned (CC BY 4.0); crypto hacks from DeFiLlama; and the number of victims posted on ransomware leak sites, from ransomware.live.

Summaries are written fresh. Follow the source links for the original reporting.

The full record is kept as a CSV file (one row per incident, refreshed every four hours) and as JSON.

Reading the chart

Bars count incidents on this page per week. The black line counts security news headlines about hacks, so you can see whether there is more news or more hacks. The purple line counts victims posted by ransomware groups, which rises with actual attacks rather than coverage.
