    About Portal View

    Every portal is a public live stream that its owner publishes on YouTube to be watched: tourist offices, ports, nature reserves, railway enthusiasts, observatories. No security cameras, no private spaces. The stream and its sound belong to the channel named under each place.

    Cameras go offline, lose daylight or get covered in snow. If a portal will not open, the page moves on by itself, and a weekly check retires streams that have stopped.

    Keys: space or right arrow for somewhere else, left arrow to go back, D daylight, M sound, S keep, F full screen.
