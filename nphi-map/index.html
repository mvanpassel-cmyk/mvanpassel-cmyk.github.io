Counts follow the search, status, type, institute and country selection above. A collaboration can fall under more than one theme. Click a theme to filter, or a country to select it.

Country grants from the Pandemic Fund, carried out by implementing entities (WHO, FAO, UNICEF, development banks) with national authorities. They are not institute-to-institute collaborations, so they are shown as a separate layer and are not counted in the totals below. Amounts are shown only where an official page states them.

What the European Commission and EU agencies fund or run in health with partner countries. Team Europe and flagships: Team Europe Initiatives, Global Gateway flagships and HERA/EU4Health actions, checked by hand. Commission country programmes: programme-level health actions of DG INTPA and DG NEAR as the Commission reports them to IATI (ongoing, or ended from 2023). EU-linked collaborations on this map: ECDC and other EU-agency work, and Team Europe or EU-funded institute collaborations, that are already in the main list (only their non-EU partner countries are marked). Amounts are EU or Team Europe amounts as stated by the source. Not counted in the totals below.

Read with care. Entries come from public web sources (project databases, institute pages, press releases) and are summarised in my own words; always check the source link. Not finding a collaboration here does not mean it does not exist. “Unclear” means the source gives no end date or is undated.
