VEKTOR NOX

DJ sampler · deep-space mixing deck

Two turntables, a crate of original loops, a mixer, FX, sample pads and a step sequencer. Drag records onto the decks, sync them up, scratch, loop and remix. You can also drop in your own loops: they stay in your browser.

Sound starts after you press the button. Headphones or decent speakers recommended.

Each row plays one pad. Press REC while the sequencer runs and hit pads: your hits land on the nearest 16th note. Everything runs on the master clock.

06 How to play

Drag a record from the crate onto deck A or B (or tap the A / B button). Press PLAY: with SYNC on, the record locks to the master BPM and drops in on the beat.

Grab the platter to scratch, drag its outer edge to nudge. Loop buttons cut the record down to 1, 2, 4 or 8 beats (or bars). Ride the EQ, filter and crossfader, then throw in FX and pads.

Your own loops: drop an audio file (WAV, MP3, OGG…) on a deck, a pad or the crate, or use Load file. Loops you made yourself work best. Files are decoded in your browser and never uploaded.

Keyboard

  • 1–4 Q–R A–F Z–V sample pads
  • G / H play deck A / B · T / Y cue A / B
  • J K L crossfader left · centre · right
  • 5–0 FX: sweep, echo, reverb, crush, warp, stutter
  • M tap tempo · [ ] BPM −/+ · P sequencer
  • Knobs: focus and use arrow keys; double-click resets.

Sound sources

All records and synth one-shots are original: they are synthesised live in your browser by this page's code (Web Audio, OfflineAudioContext). No commercial loop libraries, no copyrighted music.

Acoustic one-shots (drums, percussion, marimba, kalimba, gong…) and the two hand-percussion records use 59 samples from the Versilian Community Sample Library by Versilian Studios, released under CC0 1.0 (public domain).

Recordings you make with REC are yours.
